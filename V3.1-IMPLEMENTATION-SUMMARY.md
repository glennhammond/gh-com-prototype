# v3.1 implementation summary

Prototype `glennhammond-prototype-v3.1`, built 28 July 2026 from an untouched
copy of `glennhammond-prototype-v3`.

This is a controlled art-direction, visual-system and accessibility upgrade. It
is not a new site concept, a rebrand, a content rewrite or an architectural
redesign. The approved direction is the **Ink, Brass and Mineral** art-direction
study, combined with colour **Option A, Quiet Authority**, and the **proposed
refined colour balance**.

---

## 1. What changed, in one paragraph

Dark dropped from an environment to a punctuation mark. Brass split into a mark
colour and a text colour, which fixed a live WCAG failure. Mineral green joined
the palette as a cool counterweight. The single uniform figure treatment was
replaced by nine editorial roles whose default is narrower than the reading
column, and every figure in the site was assigned one. The case-study opening
was resequenced so the primary image no longer straddles the edge of a dark
band. Type came down at the top end and the mono scale collapsed from four
sizes to one. Nothing about the identity, the architecture, the routes, the
navigation, the page order or the copy changed.

## 3. What changed, by area

### 3.1 Colour

- Full Quiet Authority palette in `src/styles/tokens.css`, replacing the v3
  "Instrument" palette.
- Brass split into three roles: `--brass` for non-text marks, rules, focus and
  numerals; `--brass-ink` for any brass that is text; `--action-primary` with
  hover and active steps for the primary call to action.
- **This corrects a live accessibility failure.** v3 set 10.5px mono chips in
  `#A67A2E` on paper, measuring 3.42:1, which fails WCAG 1.4.3 for text at any
  size. Every one of those is now `#855619` at 5.57:1 or better.
- Mineral green added as the third voice, used for link hover, card hover and
  Layer 02.
- Three rule weights replace v3's single `--rule`.
- Semantic tokens for success, warning and error replace the one-off hexes that
  were hard-coded in `Privacy.css` and `EnquiryForm.css`.
- Legacy token names `--steel` and `--chalk` were renamed across the codebase to
  `--text-secondary` and `--on-ink-muted`, and `--ink` now means the dark
  ground rather than the current foreground. Every hard-coded `#14171c`,
  `#f3f1ea`, `#eeebe1`, `#a67a2e`, `#c9a35c`, `#8c3a2e` and `#fbf2f0` was
  removed.

### 3.2 Where dark is now used

Ink appears in exactly five places site-wide:

| Location | Justification |
|---|---|
| Homepage opening | Permitted |
| Homepage close | Permitted, and now `section--tight` |
| Case-study introduction | Permitted, shortened, metadata moved out |
| Case-study decision band | Permitted, one per case study, flagship only |
| About "how I work" | One argument band on the page |

Ink was removed from: the site footer, the case-study metadata bar, the
case-study contact block, the Practice engagement list, and the portfolio-card
evidence panel. No image sits on or across a dark band anywhere in the site.

### 3.3 Figures

The `Figure` component was rewritten around a single `role` prop. Width, mount,
padding, caption width, `sizes` and enlargement behaviour are now properties of
the role, never of the individual image.

| Role | Width | Mount | Count in site |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `primary` | 800px | bare, or plate for artwork | 12 |
| `support` | 660px | bare | 17 |
| `detail` | 480px | plate 20px | 2 |
| `pair` | 860px | one shared plate | 4 |
| `expandable` | 520px | plate, enlargement mandatory | 6 |
| `placeholder` | 660px | hatched plate | 7 |
| `portrait`, `photo` | 300px, 660 or 1180px | plate, none | 0 in case studies |
| Portfolio cover | grid cell, capped at 560px | hairline, `cover` | every card |

The old `wide` boolean is gone. The two-up evidence grid is gone: evidence now
runs as a single left-aligned column, because the grid gave every figure
identical weight, which was the uniformity complaint expressed as a layout.

Rules enforced and verified: one primary per page, never two; no run of three
or more interface overviews; the caption fixed at 58ch regardless of image
width; `cover` used in exactly one place, the portfolio card; plate padding down
from 34px to 20px, 32px for transparent artefacts.

### 3.4 Page rhythm

The case-study opening was resequenced from

  ink hero with the metadata bar inside it, then a 982px image straddling the
  band edge

to

  shortened ink introduction, pale metadata strip on plate, 800px primary
  evidence on paper, "In 60 seconds" on raised paper, narrative, evidence,
  decisions, one ink argument band, outcomes, next project on paper, contact on
  paper.

The metadata strip is the hinge. It is what stops two monumental blocks meeting.
`--section-y` came down from 64 to 148px to 56 to 116px, in proportion to the
smaller figures.

### 3.5 Typography

- Case-study title: 86px Bold to 43px Demi.
- Homepage display: 99px to 74px (v3's `clamp` topped out at 6.2rem in
  `Home.css`, above even the 5.4rem token).
- Default heading weight moved from Bold to Demi; Bold is now reserved for the
  homepage display step and the proof numerals.
- A caption step at 14px was added, distinct from small text at 15px.
- One mono size, 11px. v3 shipped 10, 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12 and 12.5px.
- Measure tightened from 66ch to 64ch; caption measure fixed at 58ch.

### 3.6 Icons

New `src/components/Icon.jsx` carries eighteen bespoke marks on a 24x24 grid,
1.5px stroke compensated by size, square terminals, no fills, no enclosing
circles, `currentColor`. In use: the four layer marks on the homepage model and
the Practice tabs, enlarge, close, previous, next, external and flagged.

Five marks (Research, Architecture, Production, Governance, Measurement) are
drawn but unused, because they have no home in the current information
architecture. This is carried over from the study as an open item rather than
resolved by inventing a page for them.

### 3.7 Accessibility

- The brass-as-text correction, described above.
- Every rendered foreground and background combination is now computed in
  `scripts/audit.mjs` (35 pairs) and the build fails if any is short. Three real
  failures were found and fixed during implementation: tertiary text on plate
  (4.11:1), brass text on plate-deep (4.38:1) and the disabled control state
  (3.99:1).
- Links no longer rely on colour: ink text with a brass underline, so they
  survive forced-colours mode. Hover shifts to mineral.
- The primary button carries a brass-light border on ink, because the action
  colour alone measures 2.4:1 against the ink ground and would fail SC 1.4.11
  for a component boundary.
- The current nav item is marked by a rule and by weight, not by colour alone.
- The enlargement dialog is capped at the asset's native width, so a 903px
  source is never presented at 1400px as though the extra pixels were detail.
- Focus is 3px brass at 3px offset on pale surfaces and brass-light on ink.

## 6. What did not change

- Site architecture, routes, page order and navigation structure.
- All 21 pages. None added, none removed.
- Every word of case-study copy, every project claim, every metric, every
  attribution, every editorial placeholder and every gap register.
  Verified by extracting all 1023 string literals from `content/projects.js`
  and diffing: the only additions are the role names themselves.
  Verified again by stripping tags from the built HTML of six representative
  pages and diffing the visible text.
- The React architecture, the framework, the routing library, the build system
  and the dependency list. No dependency was added or removed.
- The font system: ITC Avant Garde, Inter, JetBrains Mono.
- The image assets themselves. No file in `src/assets/` was touched.
- Existing accessibility behaviour that already worked: the skip link, the
  no-JS navigation fallback, the modal focus trap, `prefers-reduced-motion`,
  reserved image boxes, landmark structure and heading order.

Three strings of new interface text were added, none of which is case-study
copy:

1. Figure index numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) in the evidence label.
2. "Shown small on purpose. Enlarge to read it." on the six `expandable`
   figures. The taxonomy requires the caption to state that enlargement is
   needed; this is that statement.
3. An Enlarge control now appears on `casa-video-lightbox`, which changed role
   from a plain figure to `expandable`.

## 7. Compromises and things that need Glenn's decision

### 7.1 Decisions already taken, for confirmation

| # | Decision | Why | Reversal cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The footer moved from ink to paper** on every page | A dark footer on every page works against the 10% ink target, and on the homepage it put two ink blocks in sequence directly beneath the close. The four-colour layer spine and a strong rule now carry the terminal mark | Low. One rule in `Footer.css` |
| 2 | **The Practice engagement list moved off ink** | It was 49% of that page. A long list of routine content panels is not punctuation | Low |
| 3 | **The About "how I work" band stays on ink** | It is a short argument block, and it is that page's single dark moment. The brief permits ink for argument, and forbids it for headers, metadata, image grounds, next-project and contact blocks, none of which this is | n/a |
| 4 | **Corner registration marks are on**, on the primary figure of every case study, four 10px brass hairlines just outside the mount | Approved during implementation. Used consistently, so the mark reads as rank rather than decoration | Low. One block in `Figure.css` and one conditional in `Figure.jsx` |
| 5 | **The portfolio-card evidence panel moved from ink to plate** | It carries brass text, which measures 4.38:1 on plate-deep and 4.82:1 on plate. On ink it was also one of the "most portfolio cards" the brief moves off dark | Low |

### 7.2 Open for Glenn

1. **Homepage ink is 13.1%, against a 10% target.** The homepage is the only
   page carrying both permitted ink moments. The opening band could come down
   another 20px of padding, or the close could move to raised paper. Neither is
   obviously right. Left as is.
2. **Two `detail` figures have no authored crop.** `ws-session` and
   `isq-diff-cycle` are assigned the detail role and render at 480px, but they
   are whole screens shown small rather than crops of the decision being
   discussed. The taxonomy asks for authored crops in `scripts/images.mjs`, one
   per case study, each with a stated reason. This is the most valuable role in
   the system and it still has no purpose-made assets. See the inventory.
3. **`class-home` is an 800px source doing a primary figure's job**, on two
   pages. At 800px rendered it is exactly 1:1 and has no 2x variant. It will
   look soft on a retina display.
4. **The `casa-interview` primary figure has no caption and no evidence label
   beyond its index**, because the v3 record carries no `area` or `caption` on
   that hero. Worth a sentence.
5. **The permission risks from v3 stand unchanged.** The CASA account label
   visible in both recovered CLASS screenshots, and the ISQ student photography.
   Neither was altered here.
6. **Five icons are drawn ahead of a use.** Research, Architecture, Production,
   Governance and Measurement have no home in the current architecture.

**Not verified: a real browser.** Chromium could not be installed in the
