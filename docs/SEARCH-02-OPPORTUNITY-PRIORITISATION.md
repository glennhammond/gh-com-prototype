# Search Opportunity Prioritisation v1

**Date:** 24 August 2026  
**Workstream:** Search, Authority & Discoverability 02

## Decision principle

Priority is based on:

**relevance × evidence strength × authority potential × professional value**

Search volume is intentionally not the governing variable.

## Priority 1 — Learning platform change as a connected product/content problem

**Class:** PRIMARY AUTHORITY TERRITORY  
**Priority:** 1  
**Current evidence:** very strong

### The territory

Learning platform migration, learning-system change and platform/content transformation where information architecture, content estate, user experience and implementation cannot responsibly be treated as separate hand-offs.

### Why it is credible

Connect & Learn contains unusually clear evidence: migration from Cornerstone to an ISQ-hosted Moodle environment happened while more than sixty Storyline courses were rebuilt in the same three-month engagement. The Record explicitly explains why platform and content decisions had to remain coupled.

Historical CASA platform/front-end work broadens the pattern beyond one migration.

### SERP reality

Current results are heavily populated by:

- LMS/platform vendors;
- Moodle hosting/development specialists;
- large Moodle partners;
- migration agencies.

Some specialists explicitly stop at the platform boundary and do not design course content. Agencies that combine migration and learning design are closer comparators, but tend to present organisation-level service capability rather than an inspectable individual evidence graph.

### Whitespace

> A practitioner who can show how platform, learning architecture, course production and experience decisions affected each other — and can show the actual decision structure rather than merely claim “end-to-end”.

### Best current entry

`/work/connect-and-learn/concurrent-migration`

### Production implication

Make this Record exceptionally good as a direct-entry page. It should carry enough visible context to answer a migration-intent visitor without requiring them to navigate up to the Project first.

## Priority 2 — Digital learning product & experience architecture

**Class:** PRIMARY AUTHORITY TERRITORY  
**Priority:** 2  
**Current evidence:** very strong

### The territory

Designing digital learning/wellbeing products around real situations, useful actions, continuity and service relationships rather than defaulting to a catalogue, portal or LMS navigation model.

### Why it is credible

Wellbeing Studio 2027 contains multiple decision Records rather than a single polished outcome:

- contextual entry around moments in the working day;
- Action / Return / Explore and Useful Experience;
- connection between digital and human-led service;
- production vertical slice;
- correction of implementation decisions when product logic changed.

This is high-information-gain evidence because it shows the evolution of the product model and evidence boundaries.

### SERP reality

Search language is fragmented across product design, learning experience design, UX, digital learning strategy and experience architecture. No one phrase cleanly represents the practice.

### Whitespace

> Applying serious product and experience architecture to digital learning while remaining hands-on enough to prove the architecture in production.

### Best current entries

- `/work/wellbeing-studio/connected-service`
- `/work/wellbeing-studio/contextual-entry`
- `/practice`

### Production implication

Optimise for semantic clarity, not phrase repetition. Visible copy should explicitly name product strategy, experience architecture, contextual entry, continuity and implementation where they genuinely describe the work.

## Priority 3 — Scalable learning production systems & design systems

**Class:** EMERGING TERRITORY with a clear path to PRIMARY  
**Priority:** 3  
**Current evidence:** strong but not yet sufficiently canonicalised

### The territory

Reusable design/production systems for Rise, Storyline and broader digital-learning production: templates, components, interaction patterns, visual rules, governance and operational repeatability.

### Why it is credible

Historical CASA evidence includes a reusable Storyline production system used by the wider design team. The repository also contains an ISQ eLearning Design System case-study estate. This is more substantial than “I use templates”.

### Why it is not yet Primary

The strongest ISQ design-system evidence is still in the legacy/migration estate rather than the current four canonical THE RECORD territories. The current wildcard redirect for design-system URLs is too coarse, and one typography URL is recorded as indexed.

### Whitespace

> Design-system thinking applied to the real constraints of learning-authoring tools and ongoing learning operations, rather than a visual component library detached from production.

### Production implication

Canonicalise one strong design-system/production-system Project or Record before launch or soon after. Do not create multiple overlapping Rise/Storyline/design-system service pages.

## Priority 4 — Learning UX / platform UX

**Class:** SUPPORTING AUTHORITY TERRITORY  
**Priority:** 4  
**Current evidence:** strong

### The territory

The experience and information architecture of learning platforms — how people find, enter and use learning rather than merely how the LMS is hosted or configured.

### Evidence

- CASA LMS front-end/platform history.
- Connect & Learn platform and learning architecture.
- Wellbeing Studio contextual entry and product model.

### Search landscape

Moodle/platform specialists frequently foreground hosting, plugins, integration and infrastructure. UX is often promised as a benefit, but detailed first-hand UX reasoning is less visible.

### Whitespace

Use this as a bridge between the two primary territories, not a separate keyword-led brand.

## Priority 5 — Designing for professional judgement and defensible assessment

**Class:** SUPPORTING AUTHORITY TERRITORY  
**Priority:** 5  
**Current evidence:** very strong but narrower commercial reach

### The territory

Learning and assessment design for experienced practitioners in regulated or high-consequence environments, where judgement matters more than recall.

### Evidence

- CASA Flight Examiner Rating / examiner judgement Record.
- Assessment reasoning Artefact.
- Historical Principles of Assessment and Rules of Evidence resource with confirmed external academic/professional citation.

### Whitespace

Generic compliance-learning content is abundant. The differentiated evidence is the translation of regulation/assessment principles into reasoning structures for senior practitioners.

### Production implication

Rebuild the externally cited historical assessment resource and connect it contextually to CASA/Practice. Do not turn it into an RTO compliance lead-generation page.

## Priority 6 — xAPI / experience measurement

**Class:** EMERGING TERRITORY  
**Priority:** 6  
**Current evidence:** real but not yet sufficiently public/canonical

### The territory

Using xAPI/LRS data to describe meaningful experience and decisions rather than merely recording completion.

### Why it matters

Professional value is high and it connects naturally to systems/product practice. However, introductory xAPI content is already well covered by technical communities, vendors and standards-oriented sources.

### Promotion gate

Do not promote to PRIMARY until glennhammond.com contains first-hand evidence demonstrating several of:

- statement design/governance;
- pseudonymous identity or privacy decisions;
- reliable server-side delivery;
- LRS integration;
- validation/qualification;
- analysis that changes a product/learning decision.

A future WS Experience Intelligence Record could satisfy this much more credibly than a service page.

## Low-priority acquisition territories

### Storyline tutorials

Useful supporting evidence and potential long-tail discovery, but highly tool-bound and dominated by Articulate/community tutorial content. Retain unusually useful historical material; do not build a weekly tutorial strategy.

### Moodle how-to articles

Retain only when technically current and genuinely useful. They can contribute credibility but should not reposition Glenn as a Moodle administrator/support provider.

### Accessibility

Important quality requirement across the practice. Current evidence supports accessibility as a working principle, but the public field does not yet justify making “accessibility consultant” a primary acquisition territory.

### Multimedia/video learning design

Genuine capability and useful supporting proof. Lower strategic differentiation than the connected system/product territories.

---

## DO NOT PURSUE

- generic “what is eLearning?” content;
- generic instructional-design model definitions;
- generic learning-theory definitions;
- mass “Moodle vs X” comparison pages;
- keyword city pages;
- weekly AI-generated Storyline/Rise tips;
- FAQ farms;
- thin service pages for every technology;
- artificial “GEO/AEO” pages;
- backlink schemes, paid links or directory programmes.

## Deliberately small authority portfolio

The recommended public authority model is therefore:

1. **Learning platform + content/system transformation**
2. **Digital learning product + experience architecture**
3. **Scalable learning production/design systems**
4. supporting: **learning/platform UX**
5. supporting: **professional judgement/assessment design**
6. emerging: **xAPI / experience measurement**

Everything else should support these territories or remain evidence of breadth, not compete to become a separate positioning pillar.

## Promotion/demotion rule

A territory moves upward only when new first-hand evidence strengthens it. Search volume by itself cannot promote a territory. Conversely, a territory with low search volume may remain important if it communicates rare, valuable capability to the right professional audience.
