## 1. Architecture 02 finding

Practice is not a second portfolio, a capability catalogue, a four-layer service model or a sales page.

THE RECORD already contains the professional evidence. Practice exists to interpret what recurs across that evidence without outrunning it.

The governing relationship is:

**Project → Record → Artefact → Practice interpretation**

Project establishes territory. Record substantiates a consequential decision or way of working. Artefact demonstrates it. Practice may then name a recurring pattern only when the canonical field supports recurrence.

The previous `/practice` route does the reverse. It begins with Four Layers, promotes Rise and Storyline as parallel specialist practices, then makes four commercial engagements the dominant architecture. That structure is legacy material and is retired from the Practice route.

## 2. Formal Practice claim model

Practice claims use four editorial classifications.

### Proven recurring practice

A way of working supported by evidence across multiple canonical Project territories. A recurring claim should normally cross at least two Projects; the three primary Practice claims below are supported across all four current territories.

### Strong emerging pattern

A repeated pattern with credible evidence, but either narrower territory coverage or a contemporary formulation that would be misleading if retrospectively imposed on historical work.

### Single-project evidence

A useful practice signal currently demonstrated by one Project only. It may be discussed inside that Record but is not promoted as a recurring Practice claim.

### Aspirational positioning

A useful statement of direction, identity or career continuity that is not proven by the canonical field. It must never be presented as evidence-backed recurrence.

## 4. Explicit Practice → Record → Artefact evidence map

### Claim 01 — Start with the situation, not the inherited structure

- **Wellbeing Studio 2027** → `ws-connected-service` → `ws-connected-service-map`  
  Existing destinations were not allowed to define the future product; real working situations and useful participant experiences became the design unit.
- **ISQ Connect & Learn** → `isq-concurrent-migration` → `isq-migration-dependency-map`  
  A serial migration sequence was rejected because platform, course-estate and operational decisions needed to remain coupled.
- **CASA FERC** → `casa-examiner-judgement` → `casa-assessment-reasoning-sequence`  
  Regulation was not simply restated; the course was organised around examiner judgement and assessment reasoning.
- **TAFE Pathways** → `tafe-supported-conversation` → `tafe-facilitated-exploration`  
  A self-service default was rejected in favour of an explorable environment that supported a live careers conversation.

### Claim 02 — Keep connected decisions connected

- **Wellbeing Studio 2027** → `ws-ruok-production-slice` → `ws-ruok-production-gates`  
  Product definition, implementation, staging, reliability and later product correction remained connected rather than letting proved infrastructure become product authority.
- **ISQ Connect & Learn** → `isq-concurrent-migration` → `isq-migration-dependency-map`  
  Platform migration and more than sixty Storyline rebuilds had to teach each other inside one delivery system.
- **CASA FERC** → `casa-examiner-judgement` → `casa-assessment-reasoning-sequence`  
  Regulatory hierarchy, principles of assessment, competency reasoning, scenario design and responsive context were treated as one learning problem.
- **TAFE Pathways** → `tafe-supported-conversation` → `tafe-facilitated-exploration`  
  Facilitation, non-linear information architecture, visual exploration and stateful Storyline implementation worked as one experience rather than separate production concerns.

### Claim 03 — Solve at the altitude the problem requires

- **TAFE Pathways** → `tafe-supported-conversation` → `tafe-facilitated-exploration` — Artefact / Experience.
- **CASA FERC** → `casa-examiner-judgement` → `casa-assessment-reasoning-sequence` — Artefact / Experience / System.
- **ISQ Connect & Learn** → `isq-concurrent-migration` → `isq-migration-dependency-map` — Experience / Content / Platform.
- **Wellbeing Studio 2027** → `ws-connected-service` → `ws-connected-service-map` — Product / Experience / System.

The point is not that later work is “higher”. The field shows a practice capable of widening or narrowing its unit of design according to the problem.

## 5. Evolution-of-practice architecture

Evolution is represented as **cumulative altitude**, not a nostalgic CV timeline.

The current canonical field supports this selective sequence:

1. **2015 — TAFE Pathways:** interaction and facilitated experience.
2. **2015–21 — CASA FERC:** learning artefacts, experience and system context inside a regulator.
3. **2024 — ISQ Connect & Learn:** platform, course estate, learning architecture and operations as a coupled delivery system.
4. **2026–27 — Wellbeing Studio:** product strategy, experience architecture, service relationships and production implementation.

Earlier forms do not disappear as the practice widens. Artefact-making remains first-class inside experience and system work.

A visible integrity note must state that the canonical public Record is selective and currently begins in 2015. It does not substantiate every year of Glenn’s broader career.

## 6. Frame / Shape / Make / Evidence disposition

**Retain, with a stricter claim.**

`Frame. Shape. Make. Evidence.` remains useful because it describes four overlapping modes visible in current practice:

- **Frame** — clarify the situation, audience, constraints and actual problem.
- **Shape** — turn that understanding into product, learning, experience or system architecture.
- **Make** — prototype, build and produce at the required altitude.
- **Evidence** — distinguish intention, implementation, observation and validation; qualify what is safe to claim.

It is not presented as a sequential methodology, a service menu or terminology used by historical clients and projects. As a four-part named set it is classified as a **strong emerging pattern/current operating description**, because Evidence is most explicit in recent work.

## 7. Rise / Storyline disposition

### Storyline

Storyline has meaningful canonical recurrence across three of the four current territories:

- TAFE Pathways — non-linear, variable-heavy, application-like exploration.
- CASA FERC — interaction and course production serving assessment reasoning.
- ISQ Connect & Learn — more than sixty course rebuilds inside a platform migration.

This is enough to acknowledge Storyline as **specialist production depth**, but not enough to make a tool a top-level Practice architecture. The specialist route may remain addressable for technical/direct-entry purposes.

### Rise

Rise has useful broader practice evidence outside the current four-territory canonical field, but it does not currently have equivalent support inside this field.

The existing specialist route remains addressable, but Rise is **not promoted on `/practice`** as an equal Practice pillar. It should re-enter the core Practice interpretation only when canonical evidence earns that position.

### Shared rule

**Tools are materials, not product definitions.**

No Rise-versus-Storyline comparison belongs in the new Practice architecture.

## 9. Page and interaction composition

- Static authored composition; no tabs or filtering are required.
- Every core Practice claim has visible evidence relationships rather than hidden source logic.
- Record is the primary deep link; Artefact is the inspection link.
- Evidence relationship notes explain *why* the linked evidence supports the claim.
- Chronology is used only for selective evolution; the main Practice claims are not chronological.
- No interaction state is required for comprehension, direct entry or Back/Forward recovery.
- Motion is not required. Architectural typography, spacing, rules and evidence relationships carry the hierarchy.

## 10. Direct-entry architecture

A visitor arriving directly at `/practice` must understand within the first screen that:

- this is an interpretation of evidence;
- THE RECORD is the source of that evidence;
- Practice is not a services catalogue;
- the evidence can be inspected immediately.

No knowledge of Home, Work or the former Four Layers model may be required.

## 11. Responsive and accessibility principles

- One `h1`; section-level `h2`; claim/mode/evolution items use `h3` without skipped hierarchy.
- Native `header`, `main`-inherited route content, `section`, `article`, lists and links; no div-only pseudo-semantics for evidence.
- Claim classification is expressed in text, never colour alone.
- Evidence grids recompose to one column without changing reading order.
- No fixed-height content containers.
- Enlarged text must not require horizontal scrolling.
- Interactive targets inherit or provide at least 44px usable height.
- Focus states remain visible.
- Reduced-motion behaviour remains unaffected because Practice adds no essential motion.
- The page remains complete in statically rendered HTML without client-side JavaScript.

## 12. Editorial evidence boundaries

- Recurrence is asserted only where multiple Project territories support it.
- Historical Records retain the language supported by their source and recovered artefacts.
- Contemporary interpretive phrases are identified as interpretation, not retroactive project terminology.
- Intended Wellbeing Studio architecture is not converted into observed or validated participant outcomes.
- No outcomes, metrics or audience figures are introduced beyond canonical claims.
- A tool name cannot carry a Practice claim by itself.
- Practice does not manufacture new THE RECORD evidence to support itself.
- Commercial engagement models may exist elsewhere, but they do not define Practice.
