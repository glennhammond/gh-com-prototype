# v3.1 image-role inventory

Every image in the site, with the editorial role it has been assigned and the
treatment that role gives it.

Assigning a role is an editorial act: it is the moment somebody decides how
important a piece of evidence is. Width, mount, padding, alignment, caption
width, `sizes` and enlargement behaviour all follow from the role and are never
set on the individual image.

## How to read this

- **Desktop** is the maximum rendered width at 1440px. Every role except the
  portfolio cover is left-aligned to the reading column and grows rightwards.
- **Caption** is 58ch for every role without exception, so a wide figure and a
  narrow one carry the same reading rhythm. It is not repeated in the tables.
- **Mobile** is the behaviour below 720px. The width lever disappears there, so
  rank is carried by vertical space instead: primary 1.35x, standard 1x, detail
  and expandable 0.72x.
- **Source px** is the intrinsic size of the largest derivative in
  `src/lib/image-manifest.json`. Nothing is ever upscaled, in the page or in
  the enlargement dialog.

The homepage portrait of Glenn is photographic evidence and takes no plate. It
renders at 300px on a hairline, because it sits on raised paper and would
otherwise have no edge at all.

## Outstanding asset work

1. **Two authored detail crops are missing.** `ws-session` and `isq-diff-cycle`
   carry the `detail` role at 480px but are whole screens shown small, not crops
   of the decision the caption discusses. The taxonomy asks for one authored
   crop per case study, each with a stated reason, added to
   `scripts/images.mjs`. This is the most valuable role in the system and it has
   no purpose-made assets in the archive.
2. **`class-home` cannot carry the primary width properly.** Its source is
   800x563, so at the 800px primary width it renders 1:1 with no 2x variant. It
   is used as the primary figure on two pages, the CASA programme and CLASS.
   Either a higher-resolution capture is supplied or the role drops to
   `support`.
3. **Role-matched derivatives have not been generated.** The assets in
   `src/assets/` are still v3's 1600/800/480 ladder. `scripts/images.mjs` has
   been updated to emit 1600/1180/860/800/660/480, but sharp could not run in
   the environment this change was made in. Every `sizes` attribute already
   matches its role, so nothing over-fetches badly today. Running
   `npm run images` will emit the exact widths.
4. **The permission risks from v3 stand.** The CASA account label visible in
   both recovered CLASS screenshots must be masked, cropped or replaced before
   publication. The ISQ student photography still needs a publication permission
   and a photography licence check. Neither was altered here.
